TEST DEPLOYMENT
---------------
1. Keep the live Main Worker/domain untouched while testing.
2. Use the test Worker: pro-detailers-google-reviews-v4-test.
3. Confirm GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY exists as a Production Worker Secret.
4. Deploy with: npx wrangler deploy
5. Open the test workers.dev homepage.
6. Verify live Google Reviews.
7. Verify /api/google-reviews returns JSON.
8. Repeat /api/google-reviews and inspect X-Pro-Detailers-Review-Cache; after the first successful request, HIT indicates the cached response was served at that edge.
9. Scroll the page and verify the social rail remains fixed.
10. On a supported mobile device, tilt the phone gently and verify only the hero background moves subtly.
11. Do NOT switch the Main Worker until this test build is fully approved.
